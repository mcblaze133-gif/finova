At least two usable selected peers are required. Excel calculates min, max,
range midpoint, mean, median and quartiles for P/E, EV/EBITDA, EV/EBIT, EV/Sales
and P/S. The midpoint is explicitly a range reference. There are no invented
growth, margin, ROCE, leverage or brand-quality premiums. These cross-checks
receive zero automatic weight in the main valuation until those judgments and
the remaining comparability limitations are resolved.

## Normalisation and capital checks

Excel retains reported and adjusted P/E side by side. Exceptional items are
adjusted once; operating EBITDA already excluding an item is not adjusted twice.
HUL uses continuing earnings. Nestlé's separate earlier-year tax credit is
removed as well as its net exceptional credit. Britannia's disclosed labour-code
past-service cost has an explicit analyst add-back. Effective-tax and ownership
allocations are editable analyst proxies, not audited normalised earnings.

The narrow EV definition includes borrowings, leases and minority interests,
less cash equivalents. Strategic investments and pension/other senior claims
are excluded by that definition, not certified absent. The main cash-flow
valuation retains its separate enterprise-to-equity bridge. A fuller matched
bridge is required before treating these EV comparisons as primary valuation.

Issuer capital/allotment evidence and the exchange market-cap snapshot support
the basic share-count review, including Nestlé's already-reflected bonus and
ITC/Tata's later capital changes. Basic shares times price must reconcile within
the exchange's market-cap rounding. A current fully diluted capital audit has
not been completed; retained dilution increments in the main cases are labelled
analyst proxies.

## Verification and current developments

Private `data/peer-reviews.json` stores reviewed numeric inputs, company
selection and exact source quotations. Every build checks archive paths, hashes,
quotations, common price dates, reporting periods and accounting scope. The
numeric transcription is reviewed input, not an automated independent audit.
Private evidence and review data are not supplied by a public clone.

Registered external credit opinions are freshly requested on a fresh research
run, with access receipts and bounded timeouts. Failure does not substitute an
old archive as a new retrieval. Their issuer-derived financial observations
do not become independent corroboration merely by changing publisher domains.

Explicit documentary reviews preserve the ITC Hotels ownership/consolidation
discrepancy. Britannia's original CCPA order is visually reviewed on selected
pages. Because the PDF is scanned, a selected manual transcription is stored
with its extraction method, source URL, transcript checksum and original SHA-256. It is reusable only
after an identical original is downloaded; changed bytes invalidate it. It is
not automated OCR, a complete legal review, proof of no appeal, or verification
of an investment impact. Unresolved news remains labelled. No numerical forecast
changes are applied automatically from headlines.

PDF and website comparison results read the exact saved Excel formula values.
Tests check independent normalisation arithmetic, live input changes, missing
bridges, self-exclusion, incompatible periods, source tampering and transcript
identity. Native desktop Excel recalculation remains independently unverified.
